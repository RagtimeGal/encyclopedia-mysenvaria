The Quickening Star System is a large [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star system]] whose host star, the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/First Lessers Star|First Lessers Star]], marks the beginning of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Quickening|Quickening]]. The host star of the system was created by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Matter]], documenting their creation of all the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Elemental Gods|Elemental Gods]]. Other stars in the system were created by various other [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]], documenting the creation of their own Gods via subdivision.

The star system has a recorded 253 stars, created by a large variety of Gods.
