Death's System, whose host [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|star]] is Death's Star (published on the 26th of Spring, [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/170s BT#177 BT|177 BT]]), is used to mark the beginning of both the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War Era]] and the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Dying Period|Dying Period]]. Death's Star documents the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]] and was published by a now deceased God of which very little is known, usually simply referred to as [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Unknown Gods/Death's Star's Publisher|Death's Star's Publisher]]. The system would immediately explode with stars from Gods all across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]], discussing the ramifications of the death. On the 12th of Winter 177 BT, 256 days after the star's publication, Death's Star would fall after the passing of its publisher. The star would be republished almost immediately by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of What Is and Is Not|Gods of What Is and Is Not]], who would modify the star in unexplained ways, including moving the star's position in the sky.
# Death's Star
Death's Star was the first star ever published to have an orbit. The reason why the star's publisher decided to give it an orbit or the meaning of that orbit is unclear, but it is hypothesized it may have been to increase the star's visibility in a possibly already crowded sky. Death's Star documents the First Death vividly, although translations are torn on various details about the event based on the star's description. Most notably, astronomers are divided over whether or not the publisher of Death's Star was possibly also the perpetrator of the First Death.

The republication of Death's Star upon its publisher's passing by What Is and Is Not is believed to have caused a minor fiasco amongst the Gods, and is later credited with being part of what motivated the idea of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Greater God Conspiracy|Greater God Conspiracy]].
