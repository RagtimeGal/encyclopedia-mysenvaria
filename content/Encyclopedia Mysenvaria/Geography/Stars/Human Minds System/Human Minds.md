The Human Minds star was created by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Porous Rock|God of Porous Rock]] following their creation of humanity. The star documents their creation of humanity with the help of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Elemental Gods/God of Carbon|God of Carbon]] and the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Compound Gods/God of Hydrogen Cyanide|God of Hydrogen Cyanide]]. The star would give rise to a small system, becoming the host of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Human Minds System|Human Minds System]].
