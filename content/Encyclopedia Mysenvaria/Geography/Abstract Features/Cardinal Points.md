The cardinal points are a set of abstract points located directly on the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Edge|Edge]] of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]]. Traditionally there are four points each located at 90° intervals of one another relative to the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Central Pole|Pole]]. The first point is always in the direction of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Harmonic Treaty|Harmonic Treaty's]] orbital origin; this is north (N). The other points in order are: west (W), south (S), and east (E). Relative to the Pole each point's corresponding [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Span & Reach|span]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Azimuth, Altitude, & Distance|azimuth]] is: 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270°. 

Intermediary points refer to cardinal points somewhere between the four primary points. Intermediary points do not have true names but are instead referred to by combining the names of the primary points, always beginning with the most counter-starward point and then listing them in order of operation. For instance, north-west-north (NWN—Start at north, then halfway between north and west, then halfway between there and north) would be located at 22.5°. Whereas north-west-west (NWW—Start at north, then halfway between north and west, then halfway between there and west) would be located at 67.5°. While east-north (EN) is a proper way to refer to the point at 315°, north-east (NE) would refer to a point at 135°.

Cardinal directions are the direction which someone would need to look or move from their position on the Plane in order to face or reach the corresponding cardinal point. This means, unless standing at the Pole, to face north does not mean to face 0° relative to oneself, but instead to face the north point.
