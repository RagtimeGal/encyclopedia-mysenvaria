*Art is one of the six primary article categories of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Arts/Useful Arts/Literature/Non-fiction/Encyclopedia Mysenvaria|Encyclopedia Mysenvaria]].* 

There is no concrete definition of what art is; it encompasses a wide range of forms. Art is made by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Sophonts|sophonts]] using imagination and creativity and with purpose and intelligence. An artistic piece, work of art, or artwork, is a broad term used to describe a tangible artistic thing—a creation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Branches/Aesthetics|aesthetic]] value. Be it a plate of food, the plate itself, a theatrical play, or a song. The art made by an individual is changed by their experiences and reflects their [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Culture|culture]], itself formed by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/History|history]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Geography|geography]] of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]].

Art is commonly divided into two categories: useful arts and fine arts. The former is composed of all works which would generally be considered necessary to life and society; clothing, housing, utensils, crockery, literature, and food are some forms of useful art. The latter is composed of all works which are made for pleasure and appeal; paintings, sculptures, music, performances, and architecture.
