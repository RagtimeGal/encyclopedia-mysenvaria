Dragon waste stew was a soup commonly made across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]] typically for a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Events & Holidays/Dragon Hunt Celebration|Dragon Hunt Celebration]]. Dragon waste stew gets its name from the fact it is made using all the 'waste' product of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Dragons|dragon]] or dragons hunted following the celebration, including tough cuts of meat and unused bone and sinew.
