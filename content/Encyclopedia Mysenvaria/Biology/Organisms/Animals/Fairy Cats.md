Fairy Cats are a small group of a select few species of large cat from the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]] which have a symbiotic relationship with specific species of carnivorous [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Screaming Butterflies|screaming butterfly]]. The butterfly, which have black underwings and colorful mosaic overwings, are attracted to the cats overly-salty sweat. Male cats use the butterflies in mating displays. The butterflies are also shaken off during hunts in an effort to distract prey.
