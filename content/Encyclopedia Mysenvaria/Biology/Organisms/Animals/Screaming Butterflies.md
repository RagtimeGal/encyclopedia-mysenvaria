Screaming Butterflies are a large group of butterflies which have hard chitinous extensions extending from the bottoms of their hindwings. These chitinous parts are designed to rub against the ridged abdomen of the butterfly creating a loud chirping noise. Screaming butterflies are only found in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]], though individual specimens have been brought back to the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]] for close study.
