Masked hyper-predators are a group of large hyper-carnivorous animals from the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]], created with the express purpose of hunting [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Organisms of Mass Destruction|organisms of mass destruction]]. Individuals are equipped with inflatable sacs capable of creating air-tight seals around bodily orifices. They are also capable of mandibular separation and have shifting blade-like organs they can extend from their mandibles. Currently Mask Bears, Mask Vultures, and Mask Cats are known.
