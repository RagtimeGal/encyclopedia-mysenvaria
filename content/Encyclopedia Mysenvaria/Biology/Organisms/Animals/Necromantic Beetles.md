Necromantic beetles are a small group of avovoric beetle species which use [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|artificial stars]] to [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Necromance|necromance]] other organisms. Different species prefer different organisms but may be lenient in their choices. Male beetles attempt to impress females by showing off their ability to necromance larger individuals. They are found across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old]] and [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New Worlds]].
