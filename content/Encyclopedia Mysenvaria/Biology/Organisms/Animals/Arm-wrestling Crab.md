Arm-wrestling crab are a species of large crab known for having massive right claws which they use to wrestle each other for mates. They are found along small portions of coast in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Southern Continent|Old World]]. The crabs are important [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Culture|culturally]] as some local traditions see young men wrestle the crabs for food and sport. Individual crabs are often marked with painted shells and fought over generations.
