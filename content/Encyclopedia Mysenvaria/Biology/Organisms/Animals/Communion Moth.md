Communion moth are a domesticated species of large parasitic moth who lay their larva in the flowers of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Plants/Communion Trees|communion trees]]. The adult moths eat a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Limn|limn]]-rich diet and lay their eggs on the inside of the tree's flowers which bloom and grow into seeds encapsulating the moth's larva. The larva are equipped with [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|artificial stars]] made from the limn given to them by their parents and which allow them to communicate binary signals over long-distance.

In the wild individuals communicate information like drowning and burning. Individuals who receive these signals hop to disperse and find somewhere safer. Overtime these moths have been domesticated so that the individual in danger does not hop, but their siblings do. Larva are tapped with warmed needles allowing long-distance binary communication.
