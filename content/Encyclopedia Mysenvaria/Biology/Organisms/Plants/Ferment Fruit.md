Ferment fruit are a species of fruiting tree which grow fruit specifically designed to ferment itself. They produce fruit encased in hardy but porous shells which allow for the exchange of CO2 but not oxygen. When the fruit inside of the shell has finished fermenting the built up pressure causes the shell to crack, spilling the fermented liquids and seeds.
