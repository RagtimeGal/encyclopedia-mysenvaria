Purple Lung, also known as Mage's Lung or Wizard's Lung, is a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Biology#Disease|disease]] most often caused by the inhalation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Limn|limn]], though it may also rarely develop as a result of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Natural Personification#Natural Diffusion|natural diffusion]] of limn throughout the body. Purple Lung is characterized by the development of a persistent cough and difficulty breathing which consistently grows worse over time. It gets its name from the faint purple color and glow which victim's lungs exhibit on post-mortem examination.
