[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Entity (phenomenon)|Entities]] are physically composed of, or are themselves, materials like elements and molecules. Materials are structured in specific patterns, determined by their [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Relationships (model)|relationships]], which define their entity's attributes and properties. The most basal entities and materials are elements. With the sole exception of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Limn|limn]], everything that exists physically in Reality is made up of elements.

Elements are often divided into two groups: common elements, and uncommon elements. Common elements are those which make up the majority of entities in Reality. Generally, common elements are any element which science has readily measured. Uncommon elements are those which are confined to the footnotes of Reality. These are elements which have only been described on the rare occasion or which are only known about from the translation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]].

Molecules are constructed out of two or more elements which are bonded together chemically.
