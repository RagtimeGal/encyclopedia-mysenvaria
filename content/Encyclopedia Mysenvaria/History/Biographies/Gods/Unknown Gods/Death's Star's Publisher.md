Death's Star's Publisher is a now-deceased God for which very little information is known. They are possibly the perpetrator of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]], as is purported by some [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Branches/Astronomy|astronomers']] translations of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Death's System|Death's Star]]. It is unclear to what extent they played in the war, though it is known that they published many stars while they were alive, many of which have historically been maintained by other Gods. The total number of stars which they published is unknown.
