# Events
## 178 BT
- 58th of Winter
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/First Lessers Star|First Lessers Star]]** is published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Matter]] to document their creation of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Elemental Gods|Elemental Gods]]. The star motivates other Gods to undergo their own domain subdivision, becoming the host of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Quickening Star System|Quickening Star System]].
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Elemental Gods|The Elemental Gods]]** are created by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Matter]], beginning the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Quickening|Quickening]].
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Quickening|The Quickening]]** begins (end. 26th of Spring, [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/170s BT#177 BT|177 BT]]) following the publication of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/First Lessers Star|First Lessers Star]] by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Matter]].
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/Energy Division Star|Energy Division Star]]** is published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|God of Energy]] to document their creation of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods|Energy Gods]] inspired by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Quickening Star System/First Lessers Star|First Lessers Star]]. It becomes the second star in the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Star Systems/Quickening Star System|Quickening System]].
## 177 BT
- 26th of Spring
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era#Early-War Epoch|Early-War Epoch]]** begins (end. [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/110s BT#116 BT|116 BT]]).
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War Era]]** begins (end. [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/0s AT#0 AT|0 AT]]).
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/Dying Period|The Dying Period]]** begins (end. 90th of Spring, [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/140s BT#145 BT|145 BT]]) as a result of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]].
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|The First Death]]** occurs as the number of Gods to exist reaches its all-time peak.
- 12th of Winter — **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Death's Star System/Death's Star|Death's Star]]** is republished by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of What Is and Is Not|Gods of What Is and Is Not]] after its original publisher dies.
## 176 BT
- 1st of Summer
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Convening Star|The Convening Star]]** is published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Fulgurite|God of Fulgurite]] following the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Convening|First Convening]]. It details the organization of the event, its attendees, the discussions held, and the conclusions drawn therein.
  - **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Convening|The First Convening]]** is held by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Phenomena Gods/Gods of Earth/God of Fulgurite|God of Fulgurite]]. 20 [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Gods|Gods]], all of whom witnessed the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Death|First Death]], are invited however only 16 arrive. The convening sees the formation of many ideas and theories regarding the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/God-War Era|God-War]].
- 3rd of Summer — **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Afterlife System/Afterlife Star|The Afterlife Star]]** is published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Energy Gods/God of Chromodynamic Energy|God of Chromodynamic Energy]] following the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/God-War Era/First Convening|First Convening]]. It makes public many of their ideas surrounding the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Afterlife|afterlife]].
## 175 BT
- 33rd of Autumn — **[[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Spatial Act|The Spatial Act]]** is passed and published by the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Biographies/Gods/Gods of Matter, Energy, Time, and Space|Gods of Time and Space]], it severely limits Gods' abilities to manipulate time and space..
