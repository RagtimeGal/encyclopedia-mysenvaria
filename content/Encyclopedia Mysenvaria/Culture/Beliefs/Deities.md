A deity is a speculative being who exerts some level of authority over the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Models/Universal Strata Model|Nature]], especially over matters regarding life, death, and the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Culture/Beliefs/Afterlife|afterlife]]. Cultural beliefs vary regarding the number and function of deities but they are most often believed to oversee an afterlife if one is speculated. Besides the afterlife they are also often associated with the creation of Reality or other higher levels of the universe. Deities are first recorded in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Phenomena/Stars|stars]] following the creation of [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Human|humans]] in [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Indexes/History/Decades/50s BT#56 BT|56 BT]].
