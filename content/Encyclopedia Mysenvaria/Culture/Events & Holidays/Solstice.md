Solstice occur as the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Stars/Harmonic Treaty|Harmonic Treaty]] passes over a portion of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Plane|Plane]] at its furthest or closest point. This occurs for each point across the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Abstract Features/Harmony Line|Harmony Line]] twice a year, as the summer solstice (at its closest) and winter solstice (at its furthest). These times have been historically celebrated by [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Science/Technologies/Agriculture|agriculturally]] dependent societies as they symbolize the start and end of the harvest.
