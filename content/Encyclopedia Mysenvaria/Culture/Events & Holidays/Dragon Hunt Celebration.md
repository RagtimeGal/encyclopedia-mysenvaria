Dragon hunt celebrations are celebratory events held following the successful hunt of a [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Biology/Organisms/Animals/Dragons|dragon]]. They were especially common in the late [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/History/Golden Age/Golden Age|Golden Age]] but are still held in rural areas. They have also seen a small revival with the colonization of the [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Geography/Natural Features/Northern Continent|New World]]. Traditionally [[Encyclopedia Mysenvaria/Arts/Useful Arts/Cuisine/Dragon Waste Stew|Dragon Waste Stew]] is made and consumed during these celebrations.
