# Writer's Note
My current understanding of Mysenvar's cosmology is that it completely mirrors our own. (With the fantastical elements of the Real and Unreal added, of course.) The Real is just everything physical (down to quarks), and the Unreal are the laws/math that model how the physical interacts. Material is the equivalent to atoms, I believe that to be clear through the current page's short paragraph description on the topic.

I'm not entirely sure on the direction I want to take this at the moment (How progressed is the Mysenvarian understanding of particle physics?), and I'm going to leave it to ruminate until the setting's history is further developed. At the moment I think modern Mysenvar's knowledge is roughly analogous to mid-19th century American and British comprehension of chemistry, but I don't want to lock myself into that decision.

Mysenvarian cultures probably have a pretty good grasp on chemistry and fundamentals of the universe, I believe. Gods gifted man stars, and stars require a very good understanding of Reality to properly make them work. (Although basic stars might be the most powerful, that's a bit of a tangent I'll have to deal with not too long from when I'm writing this.)
