> The following article is metatextual. It provides information on what the *Encyclopedia Mysenvaria* is, how it should be used, how you can use it, and how you can help improve it.

This Code of Conduct serves to protect the integrity of Mysenvar, the *Encyclopedia Mysenvaria*, and its community. By contributing to the community, for instance by submitting [GitHub issues](https://github.com/RagtimeGal/encyclopedia-mysenvaria/issues/new/choose), writing comments, or by participating in community spaces, you agree to abide by these rules. If you do not agree to follow these rules or fail to abide by them you may be stripped of your ability to participate in the community.
## Standards
Expected behavior in the community includes:
- Constructive disagreements. It's OK to have differing opinions on creative decisions and topics, so long as those disagreements remain civil.
- Keeping interactions free of personal attacks, passive aggression, and drama.
- Remaining on topic.

Unacceptable behavior in the community includes, but is not limited to:
- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind.
- Plagiarizing content or passing off others' work as your own.
- Spam, trolling, or low-effort disruption.
- Attempting to override or subvert decisions made by moderators.
## Enforcement
Violations of the Code of Conduct are taken seriously. Violations may result in:
- A warning.
- Being blocked from filing issues or submitting comments.
- Being removed from community spaces altogether.
