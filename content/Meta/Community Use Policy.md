> The following article is metatextual. It provides information on what the *Encyclopedia Mysenvaria* is, how it should be used, how you can use it, and how you can help improve it.

> [!example] TL;DR
> Make fan art, write fan fiction, translate, quote, and talk about the *Mysenvaria* and Mysenvar as much as you would like, so long as it's free and clearly unofficial. Don't republish the *Mysenvaria* as your own, don't sell anything based on it without permission, and don't feed it to AI.

There is no one way to use the *Mysenvaria*, it's up to you to define what 'using' it means to you. Perhaps you simply enjoy browsing the pages and reading to immerse yourself in a fantasy world? Maybe you're looking for specific ideas or concepts you can borrow for your own setting? You could be looking for a new story to write! Or trying to find the perfect place to start a TTRPG campaign? 

I encourage you to share anything you make ("Projects") within Mysenvar (the intellectual property, "IP") or using the *Mysenvaria*! It's awesome to see people do stuff with my setting! I only ask that if you do so your project follows the rules as they're set forth in this policy. If your project is found to be in violation of any of these rules I reserve the right to shut it down. By sharing a project publicly you're OK with me linking to or showcasing it, with credit!

If something is not covered here, ask me before doing it!
# The Rules
- **The project must be free!**
	- You cannot require any form of transaction (not just monetary transactions!) for someone to gain access to your project. This means no surveys, unrelated downloads, opting into unnecessary email lists, or requiring other nuisance actions.
	- You are allowed to accept donations and generate ad revenue.
	- You are allowed to have paid sponsorships of your content, so long as it is clearly identified to the audience.
	- If you're interested in making a monetized project, reach out to me!
- **The project must be clearly unofficial!**
	- You may not say or imply that I (Ragno), Mysenvar, or the *Encyclopedia Mysenvaria* recognizes, endorses, or sponsors your project in any way.
	- You are required to state someplace that the project is not official. The wording of this doesn't matter too much, so long as it's legible and placed somewhere easily accessible.
	- This also means you cannot use official logos.
- **The project must not reproduce content 1:1!**
	- Your project must use Mysenvar and the *Encyclopedia Mysenvaria* in a creative way! You may not reproduce content already in the IP without adding something new to it!
	- This does not mean you may not quote the *Mysenvaria* or use its images, so long as they do not make up the majority of the project.
- **No generative AI!**
	- You may not train, fine-tune, or prompt generative AI based on any of the *Mysenvaria*'s contents.
- **The project must be lawful!** 
	- These rules are in addition to the laws of your land.
- **The license granted to you by this policy may be revoked at any time!**
	- I reserve the right to shut your project down whether or not it is found in violation of this policy.
# The Cans
So, with the rules laid out, what kinds of projects are you allowed to make? Well, pretty much anything, so long as it's free! Make art of characters, write stories, post some videos, or draw maps!

The *Mysenvaria* has big potential to be used for making tabletop campaigns and homebrew! Play them privately, or share them with the world!

Besides that the policy also allows you to translate articles of the *Mysenvaria* into other languages, so long as the translation:
- is free to read,
- is clearly marked as an unofficial translation, and
- links back to the original article.

If you're planning to translate a large portion of the *Mysenvaria* I'd love to hear about it! Feel free to reach out.
